Vixxing README hero: generated subject layer
==============================================

Communication job: the telephone call is the attack surface Vixxing protects.
The handset sits in the reserved area of hero-layout.svg (x 760-1160, y 24-270
of a 1200x420 canvas) and replaces the vector placeholder group id="subject".
Title text, the code cells, and "Caller Verified" stay in SVG and must NOT be
generated.

Prompt
------
A single classic corded desk-telephone handset, studio product render, floating
at a diagonal: mouthpiece at lower left, earpiece at upper right, tilted about
28 degrees, seen slightly from above at three-quarter view. Matte charcoal
plastic (#1C222B to #2A313C), softly rounded, minimal and modern, no brand
marks. A short, tight coil of matching charcoal cord hangs from the mouthpiece
end and stays fully inside the frame. Lighting: dark, low-key studio light with
a thin, crisp emerald-green rim light (#2EA66A) tracing the top edge of the
handset, as if lit by a status light. Subtle, realistic surface sheen; no
glow haze, no lens flare.

Composition: landscape 16:10, handset centered, occupying about 80% of the
frame width, with generous even padding on all sides so no part touches an
edge.

Background: a perfectly flat, solid magenta (#FF00FF) background with no
gradient, texture, floor, reflection, cast shadow, or vignette. Do not use any
magenta or pink on the handset itself.

Constraints: one isolated object only. No text, no letters, no numbers, no
keypad, no screen, no logo, no watermark, no hands, no people, no desk.

Notes for the composer
----------------------
- Key color is magenta (not green) because the subject has a green rim light.
- Upload the image as generated (PNG preferred, at least 1600px wide). The
  magenta is removed, the transparent cutout is saved as source/hero-subject.png,
  composited into hero-layout.svg, and exported as assets/readme/hero.png.

Result
------
Two candidates were generated; option 2 (strong green rim, long fading cord)
was used. Cutout: source/hero-subject.png. Published: assets/readme/hero.png.
