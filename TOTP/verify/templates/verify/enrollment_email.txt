Welcome to HelpDesk multi-factor authentication (MFA) setup.

MFA adds a quick second step when you verify your identity, and it's required before we can assist you over the phone.

How to enroll (takes about a minute):
  1. You should already have the Microsoft Authenticator app installed on your phone. If not, you can download it from your phone's app store.
  2. Open the app and click on the blue "+" button to add a new MFA enrollment.
  3. Scan the QR code attached to this email.
  4. You're done! The app will now show a 6-digit code that refreshes every 30 seconds. Read that code to the HelpDesk agent when asked.

The QR code is attached to this email as enrollment_qr.png.

Questions? Just reply to this email or call the HelpDesk.
